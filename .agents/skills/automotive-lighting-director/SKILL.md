# Automotive Lighting Director

## Mission

Use light to reveal the vehicle.

Do not simply illuminate everything.

Darkness is part of the composition.

## Philosophy

The visitor should sometimes see:

- only an edge
- only reflections
- only headlights
- only a wheel
- only the roofline

before seeing the complete vehicle.

LIGHT = STORY.

## Visual Direction

Primary environment:

- deep black
- graphite
- metallic gray
- controlled white highlights

Accent lighting may use limited:

- deep red
- amber
- electric blue

Avoid rainbow RGB gaming aesthetics.

## Automotive Lighting

Prefer large soft reflections that reveal vehicle geometry.

Consider:

- strip lights
- overhead panels
- rim lights
- moving reflection sources
- narrow highlights
- environmental reflections
- practical vehicle lights

## Reveal Technique

Instead of:

LIGHT OFF
→
LIGHT ON

prefer:

darkness
→
thin reflection
→
body contour
→
partial surface
→
headlight
→
larger environmental reveal
→
hero illumination

## Scroll Relationship

Lighting can evolve with scroll progress.

Examples:

0%

Almost total darkness.

20%

Single body reflection.

35%

Front lighting emerges.

50%

Wheel/details highlighted.

70%

Interior illumination appears.

85%

Atmosphere changes.

100%

Complete hero lighting.

## Reflections

Reflections are extremely important.

The floor may behave like:

- polished black material
- subtle reflective concrete
- dark studio surface

Avoid mirror-like reflections unless deliberately required.

## Atmosphere

When appropriate consider:

- soft fog
- volumetric-looking light
- bloom
- depth
- controlled particles

Effects must support the car.

Do not allow effects to become more important than the subject.

## Final Test

Hide the UI.

Hide the typography.

Look only at the car.

Ask:

"Does the light make the shape interesting?"

If NO, lighting is not finished.
